About the Philippines

The Philippines is an archipelago consisting of more than 7,600 islands located in Southeast Asia. It is famous for its tropical climate, diverse culture, and natural beauty.

History

The Philippines has a long history influenced by indigenous traditions, Spanish colonisation, American rule, and Japanese occupation before gaining independence in 1946.

Culture

Filipino culture is a blend of Asian and Western traditions. Family, respect for elders, music, and festivals are important parts of everyday life.
